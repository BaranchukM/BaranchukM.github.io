Hey there! I'm Mikhail - or Mike for short - a Kubernetes Engine dev @ Google who loves solving challenging problems and understanding the big picture. My research interests are about building multi-agent systems of AI and modeling complex/dynamic systems, using graph neural networks, and keeping AI safe and secure. I've won research grants and presented some cool research at NeurIPS such as how AIs might be able to secretely collude by hiding information with perfect information-theoretic security. During my time at Oxford, I began building models to forecast FX and optimize my exchanges as the European energy crisis hit and central banks were raising rates. This real-world challenge sparked my deep interest in macroeconomics and trading, and motivating my ongoing research in complexity economics. 

My experience spans cutting-edge biometric security, fraud detection, and state-of-the-art agentic AI, working with firms from early-stage startups to industry leaders like Microsoft. On the finance side, I’ve also built novel modeling frameworks for the National Bank of Canada & developed software to optimize risk models at Morgan Stanley. Sometimes I take part in hackathons and trading competitions like Optiver’s Trading at Close and IMC’s Prosperity Trading Challenge, where my team finished 14th out of 7600+. Whether it’s AI alignment, economics, or the latest F1 drama, I’m always up for a good chat. Feel free to reach out!
